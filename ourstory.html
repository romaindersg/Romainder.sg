Our Story

The Journey

ROMAINDER began as a youth-led initiative focused on tackling one of the world’s most overlooked environmental challenges: food waste. By combining youth leadership with community collaboration, the organization empowers students to design and lead sustainability initiatives within their own communities.

Through partnerships with farms, community organisations, and global sustainability networks, ROMAINDER diverts food waste and transforms it into opportunities for education, innovation, and environmental action.

In 2025, ROMAINDER embarked on a new journey; Sustainability - Starting with Us. Our initiatives shifted towards community involvement, allowing youth and elderly alike to take part in our global movement.

OUR MISSION

To empower our community to reduce food waste through education, innovation, and community action.

OUR VISION

A world where sustainable food practices are driven by youth leadership and community collaboration, starting with US.
